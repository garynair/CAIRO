> Live pass complete 2026-09-08. 26 in-window postings sampled across the equivalent-title set (Chief AI Risk Officer / Chief Data & AI Officer / Group Head of AI / Head of AI / VP of AI / VP-SVP AI Transformation / VP Head of AI CoE / Head of AI-First Development / Head of AI Solutions, and the federal CAIO / CDAIO shape mandated under OMB M-25-21). All 12 existing modules were probed against the sample and every confirmed requirement theme maps onto an existing module. **Zero additions this cycle** — see the internal curriculum plan.
>
> Continuity-bias thresholds: net-new content requires ≥ 3 postings in-window AND ≥ 30 percent frequency AND no existing module can be incrementally extended. No emerging theme cleared all three this cycle.

## Ownership rule (this role)

Chief AI Risk Officer (level 70) owns the **CEO-scope AI agenda**: enterprise AI strategy, AI portfolio P&L attribution, board-visible AI ROI reporting, external positioning at CEO / board / press / voluntary-code scope, enterprise AI budget envelope, AI-first workforce transformation (with CHRO), AI investment-case authoring to CFO and board, AI Governance Board / federal CAIO Council leadership (under OMB M-25-21), enterprise build-vs-buy at portfolio scope, and cross-functional partnership with CFO / CIO / CTO / CDO / CHRO / GC / CRO / CISO / CMO / CPO / CMO(medical) / Chief Scientific Officer.

Defers *down* to [head-of-ai-governance-learning](../head-of-ai-governance-learning) at level 60 for leadership execution of the governance function (running the AI Governance Council, ISO 42001 AIMS operating calendar, regulator-facing engagement, governance-function hiring, governance-function P&L). Defers *down* to [senior-ai-governance-architect-learning](../senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) at level 50 for architectural design authority. Defers *down* to [ai-evaluation-engineer-learning](../ai-evaluation-engineer-learning), [ai-risk-engineer-learning](../ai-risk-engineer-learning), and [ai-governance-analyst-learning](../ai-governance-analyst-learning) for hands-on assurance / risk-engineering / analyst legwork. Defers *sideways* to [agentic-safety-engineer-learning](../agentic-safety-engineer-learning) for frontier-agent methodology depth and to [security-learning](../security-learning) for deep ML / AI security. Defers *out of scope* to General Counsel / Chief Privacy Officer / Chief Risk Officer / Chief Compliance Officer / Chief Audit Executive / CFO for their respective enterprise mandates.

Author enterprise AI strategy: three-year vision, defensive-and-offensive posture, portfolio prioritization by value and feasibility, alignment with corporate strategy and CFO-approved capital plan. Not project-list assembly — a strategy document that lands at board level and sets the enterprise's public AI positioning.
